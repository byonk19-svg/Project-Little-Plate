# Private ten-food pilot review packet

**Status:** reviewer intake only — not an approval or publication record

This packet assembles the ten candidate identities already named by the PRD
and gives a qualified reviewer one place to record the evidence required by the
private-pilot scope. It contains no preparation, serving, allergen,
developmental, nutrition, storage, medical, or visual-rights guidance.

Do not fill a blank with a guess. Use `Insufficient evidence` or `Block` when
the qualified evidence is missing, and leave the candidate unavailable. Do not
store reviewer names, contact details, credentials, birthdates, medical notes,
reaction histories, or caregiver notes.

## Packet envelope

- Package identifier: `REQUIRED_REVIEWER_INPUT`
- Package version: `REQUIRED_REVIEWER_INPUT`
- Candidate count: `10` (fixed)
- Candidate classification: `production_candidate` (must be verified by the
  import boundary)
- Qualified approval references: `REQUIRED_REVIEWER_INPUT`
- Review date(s): `REQUIRED_REVIEWER_INPUT`
- Release owner role: `REQUIRED_REVIEWER_INPUT`
- Decision record location: `REQUIRED_REVIEWER_INPUT`

The packet is not complete until every applicable row below has a qualified
authority reference, evidence location, decision, review date, and follow-up
state. A completed packet still requires the controlled import and publication
gates.

## Detailed review form

For concerns, proposed changes, or reviewer notes, complete the matching
section in [catalog-review-form.template.md](./catalog-review-form.template.md)
for the candidate and dimension. Quote candidate values verbatim and keep
proposed changes separate. Owner adjudication belongs in the repository's
append-only adjudication boundary, never in this reviewer packet.

## Fail-closed completion rule

The packet is not import-ready when any required field is
`REQUIRED_REVIEWER_INPUT`, any evidence is unsupported, any authority reference
is missing, any decision is `Block`, `Revise`, or `Insufficient evidence`, or a
conditional visual requirement has not been resolved. A private pilot does not
waive these conditions, and this packet cannot open external caregiver access.
